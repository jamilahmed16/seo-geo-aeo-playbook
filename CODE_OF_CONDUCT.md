# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.

## Our standard

Be respectful and constructive. Welcome different levels of experience. Give and accept feedback with care. Focus on what helps the community and the work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing others' private information, and spam or self-promotion unrelated to the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits and issues that do not follow this code, and may restrict participation for repeated violations.

Report concerns through [jamilahmed.com](https://jamilahmed.com). Reports are handled privately.
